Legacy & Vision

Building Landmarks.
Creating Legacies.

Neopolis embodies a warm, modern architectural ethos. Conceived with the design precision of leading global residential landmarks in Dubai, London, and Singapore, every detail prioritizes sweeping views across the tranquil waters of Gandipet Lake.

Soaring 55 stories above Neopolis, the twin towers feature expansive floor-to-ceiling glass facades, private sky terraces, and fluid sanctuary spaces that fuse resort hospitality with refined domestic comfort.
